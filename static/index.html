How to read CTR

CTR is the share of thumbnail impressions that became a click. Low CTR points at the title/thumbnail; low retention (Audience tab) points at the content itself.

It falls naturally when YouTube shows a video to a broader audience, so a CTR drop alongside an impressions rise is not automatically bad.

Videos with fewer than 100 impressions are left out of the rankings: their CTR is mostly noise.
